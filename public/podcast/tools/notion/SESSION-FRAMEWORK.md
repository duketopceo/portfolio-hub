# Episode framework (every show)

Each episode is **five beats** on one row/card:

1. **Science** — exactly **one** primary thread, tagged by **Science pillar** (`Nuclear` *or* `Chemistry` *or* `Rockets`). Do not blend all three in one episode unless it is one story (e.g. rocket propellant chemistry); the pillar is the *lens*.
2. **Tech** — tools, infra, AI, fabs, software, or hardware product story.
3. **History** — precedent, policy arc, war / industrial / scientific history that reframes the news.
4. **Founder** — a person, operator, or leadership moment worth a dedicated segment.
