# Ep 9 — Master research brief (one shot)

You are briefing a host for a single long-form episode. Prioritize **primary sources, filings, company posts, regulator dockets, and named journalism** over generic blogs. For each claim, note the source type. If something is uncertain, say so.

Cover these beats in one flowing brief (with section headers):

1. **Nuclear / grid / SMRs** — U.S. and allied SMR timelines, utility PPAs announced in the last ~18 months, NRC / international regulator headlines that matter for deployment (not encyclopedia history unless it explains a 2025–2026 decision). Utah / western interconnect angles if material.

2. **Tech** — Enterprise adoption of AI coding tools: cite any **public** statements, job posts, or conference lines about AI-assisted software delivery at scale. Separately: one “hardware or infra” thread (e.g. advanced packaging, fab geography, power-per-rack) tied to headlines from the last 90 days.

3. **History** — One tight parallel (e.g. Cold War procurement, ARPANET moment, or energy crisis policy) that reframes **today’s** nuclear or tech story—two paragraphs max, must land on “why this matters this week.”

4. **Founder** — Profile one operator or executive in the news: career arc, incentives, what they’re optimizing for, and one contrarian read.

End with **10 suggested cold-open lines** (one sentence each, no clichés) and **6 risks / counterarguments** the host should acknowledge.
